# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list useful. A few ground rules keep it fair and clean.

## What belongs here

- Resources about sports betting or prediction market **data**: APIs, datasets, open-source tools, modeling education, agent tooling, communities, and responsible gambling resources.
- Commercial products are welcome, including products that compete with each other or with anything already listed.
- Responsible gambling resources are always welcome.

## What does not belong here

- Picks, touts, tipster services, or anything selling predictions.
- Affiliate links, referral links, or tracking parameters of any kind.
- Dead links, parked domains, or projects that have been archived or abandoned.
- Marketing superlatives. "The best", "the cheapest", and friends will be edited out.

## Entry format

- One line per entry: `- [Name](https://example.com/) - Short neutral description ending with a period.`
- Keep entries in **alphabetical order** within their section.
- Descriptions state what the thing is, not how great it is. Specific claims (coverage counts, pricing) should come from the linked page itself.
- Link the canonical page (homepage, docs, or repo), not a blog post about it.

## Self-promotion and conflicts of interest

Submitting your own project is fine and normal. Two requirements:

1. Say so in the pull request ("I built this").
2. Accept the same neutral one-line treatment as every other entry.

The maintainer of this list runs ParlayAPI, one of the listed vendors; see the Disclosure section of the readme. PRs that improve competitor entries are explicitly welcome and will not be buried.

## Checks

- Verify your link loads before submitting.
- Run `npx awesome-lint` locally if you can; CI runs it on every PR.
